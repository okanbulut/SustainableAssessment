## What students do

Students prepare an employment and on-the-job support plan for the fictional case discussed in class, grounded in supported employment principles and the case information. The plan covers the person's preferences, strengths and support needs; a proposed job and why it fits; a step-by-step task analysis of one job task; accommodations, instruction and workplace supports; the roles of the job coach, employee, employer and co-workers; how natural supports will be built and the criteria for fading job coach support; and indicators for monitoring job adjustment and task performance. Students also include an excerpt from an AI-generated suggestion for the case with a critique of its fit to the case, a brief process record and an AI use declaration. The task ends with a five-minute oral explanation.


## What AI may be used for

AI may be used only to generate ideas about the fictional case or to review a draft plan. Do not enter identifying information about real people into AI tools. AI suggestions do not replace professional judgement; evaluate and justify them against the case information. Include a short excerpt from an AI suggestion for the case and your critique of it. Your declaration records the tool(s), your purpose, and an assumption or omission you found in the suggestion and the change you made. The declaration is worth 5 of 100 points.

## How it's marked

Out of 100 points:

- Person-centred approach and rationale for the job match: 20
- Quality of the job task analysis and support plan: 20
- Natural supports and plan for fading job coach support: 18
- Clarity of workplace stakeholders' roles: 12
- Monitoring indicators and decision criteria: 10
- Critique of the AI suggestion in light of the case information: 5
- Clarity and accuracy of the AI use declaration: 5
- Process record showing drafts, decisions and changes: 5
- Defence of planning decisions in the five-minute oral explanation: 5

Twenty points reward process and judgement (AI critique, declaration, process record and oral defence); the rest goes to the plan itself. The oral asks why the job suits the person, which indicators will show the support is working, and what assumption or omission the student found in the AI suggestion. The oral adds about five minutes of contact time per student.
